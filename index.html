A few useful details

Before we
add a dimension.

Which products are you focusing on?

Our initial commercial focus is footwear and accessories. We review product suitability with you before agreeing on a pilot, including surfaces, size and the detail needed for your application.

What would a pilot include?

A pilot starts with an agreed product set, intended uses and acceptance criteria. We scope the capture, reconstruction, deliverables and commercial terms together before work begins.

Can I deploy your robot at my location?

Our initial offering is managed scanning through pilot projects. Customer-operated robot subscriptions and wider hardware support are planned for later stages.

Are the examples interactive 3D models?

The examples on this site are recorded views of actual prototype reconstructions. They are videos, rather than interactive viewers. Asset formats and delivery requirements are agreed as part of each pilot.
