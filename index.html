What we build

A robotic product digitization pipeline—from capture to clean 3D assets.

Autonomous capture

Robot-guided camera motion, structured viewpoints, and repeatable lighting for consistent scans.

3D reconstruction

High-detail 3D reconstruction with reliable scale and quality checks.

Asset delivery

Clean mesh, optional materials, turntable renders, and QC metrics—ready for web and ecommerce pipelines.

How it works

A simple, reliable loop designed for real-world production.

1
Capture

Robot executes viewpoints and collects images/depth reliably.

2
Reconstruct

Build geometry, calibrate scale, and validate coverage.

3
Refine

Denoise, fill holes, optimize mesh, generate deliverables.

4
Publish

Export asset bundle + QC report for downstream use.
